---
name: LEVEL UP product principles
description: Product positioning and child-centered engagement constraints for LEVEL UP.
---

LEVEL UP serves students ages 5–17 as an interactive learning layer, not a replacement for schools, teachers, textbooks, or classrooms. Gamification should reward meaningful learning and avoid endless-use or pressure mechanics.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the product's positioning and safety principles in the project brief.

**How to apply:** Keep product copy positive about education, prioritize clarity and age-appropriate learning, and make progress mechanics encouraging rather than manipulative.
